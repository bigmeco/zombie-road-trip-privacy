Zedshift — iPhone Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy describes Zedshift for iPhone (bundle ID com.bigmeco.zombieroadtrip), developed by Nikolai Ilin / BigMeco. The game is free, includes optional rewarded advertising, and has no in-app purchases in this release.

Data on your iPhone

The game stores your best distance, audio volume, selected and unlocked cars, bonus inventory and daily trial progress locally. These records are not uploaded to a developer-owned server. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups are managed by Apple.

Optional advertising

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) provides advertisements that you choose to watch for an in-game reward. Google's SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device identifiers, app and advertising interactions, crash reports and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. The game does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests and manages consent where required. When required, you can reopen advertising privacy choices from the game's pause menu: Privacy Settings → Advertising privacy settings. Tracking permission is requested separately through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can refuse tracking and continue playing. Identifier availability and ad personalization depend on your consent, iPhone settings and applicable rules. We do not require permission or consent as a condition of playing.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads iOS data disclosure.

Game Center leaderboards

Online rankings use Apple's Game Center. Opening the ranking may ask you to sign in to Game Center. When signed in, the game submits the distance of a completed ordinary run to Apple and reads leaderboard names, game-specific player identifiers and scores to display rankings. Daily car trials remain separate from ordinary run records. Your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players. The game does not receive your Apple Account password or payment details, does not read your contacts and does not operate its own account server.

Playing an ordinary run does not require signing in to Game Center. Manage your Game Center profile, sharing and account choices in iPhone Settings. Apple's retention and deletion controls apply to information stored in Game Center; uninstalling this game alone does not delete Game Center records. See Game Center & Privacy.

Sharing, security and retention

We do not sell personal data. The third-party services described above process data for their stated purposes, including advertising which may involve tracking when authorized. Network communication uses platform and SDK security protections. Local progress remains until deleted with the app. Google and Apple retain service data according to their own policies. If you email support, we use your email and message to respond; do not send passwords or sensitive account information.

Children and your choices

Zedshift is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. App Store age ratings may differ by country and operating system. Rewarded advertisements and Game Center participation are optional. Tracking choices are available in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The privacy policy is accessible from the game's pause menu.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when features or data practices change. Questions and data-related requests: bigmecoprog@gmail.com.
